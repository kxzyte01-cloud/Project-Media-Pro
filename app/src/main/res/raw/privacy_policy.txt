DRAFT PRIVACY POLICY (Owner must review before publishing)

Project Media Pro operates completely offline on your device.

1. Data Collection: Project Media Pro does not collect, record, or transmit any personal information, usage telemetry, analytics, or crash reports.
2. Network Access: The application does not request the Android INTERNET permission and cannot connect to any remote server.
3. Media and Projects: All video, audio, image files, and project templates stay strictly in your device storage under your control.
4. Backups: Automatic cloud backup is disabled (allowBackup="false").
