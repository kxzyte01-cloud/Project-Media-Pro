# Privacy Policy (DRAFT — Owner must review before publishing)

> **Status:** Plain-English DRAFT. The repository owner must review and approve this text before publishing the application to any app store.

Project Media Pro is designed from the ground up to protect your privacy. The app works completely offline on your Android device.

## 1. We Collect and Send Nothing
Project Media Pro does not collect, store on external servers, or transmit any personal data, device identifiers, usage analytics, telemetry, or crash reports.

## 2. No Internet Access
The application does not request the Android `INTERNET` permission. It is technically incapable of connecting to the internet, downloading remote assets, or communicating with any server.

## 3. Your Media Stays on Your Device
All videos, audio clips, images, templates, and project files you create or edit remain strictly on your device's local storage under your control.

## 4. No Cloud Backups or Third-Party SDKs
Automatic Android cloud backup is disabled (`android:allowBackup="false"`), and the app contains zero advertising, analytics, or tracking libraries.
