# Privacy Policy — Project Media Pro

Project Media Pro is designed from the ground up to protect your privacy. Video editing, playback, captions, and export run 100% offline on your Android device.

## 1. We Collect and Send Nothing
Project Media Pro does not collect, store on external servers, or transmit any personal data, device identifiers, usage analytics, telemetry, ads, or crash reports.

## 2. Optional HTTPS Model Downloads Only
Editing, preview, and export work completely offline. The Android `INTERNET` permission is used **exclusively** when you explicitly choose to download an optional Open Whisper speech-to-text model in Settings over encrypted HTTPS (`usesCleartextTraffic="false"`). The app never performs background network requests or uploads user media.

## 3. Your Media Stays on Your Device
All videos, audio clips, images, captions, templates, and project files you create or edit remain strictly on your device's local storage under your control.

## 4. No Cloud Backups or Third-Party SDKs
Automatic Android cloud backup is disabled (`android:allowBackup="false"`), and the app contains zero advertising, analytics, or tracking libraries.
